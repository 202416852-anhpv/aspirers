Source: https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=10012217 (Intellectual Property Policy), https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=10012218 (Anti-Counterfeit & Knockoff Policy), https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=10012219 (Product Listing & Content Policy) — compiled from public TikTok Shop Academy content, last_updated: 2026-08-22.

- Intellectual Property Policy (Risk: Critical) — TikTok Shop applies a strict IP-management regime, banning unauthorized use of trademarks, copyrighted assets, design patents, AND publicity/personality rights.
  - Avoid: portrait/name/signature of a celebrity, influencer, or streamer; brand logos, sports-team emblems, movie/anime franchise IP; using someone else's TikTok video footage without permission.
  - Safer: original graphics created in-house; short, generic slogans that fall outside any trademark category.
  - Enforcement: listing removal, violation points deducted, selling privileges temporarily locked, payout freeze, permanent account suspension.
- Anti-Counterfeit & Knockoff Policy (Risk: Critical) — an absolute ban on counterfeits AND knockoffs (designs that intentionally mimic another brand's silhouette/pattern/lines even without the logo attached).
  - Avoid: bags/apparel copying an exclusive monogram pattern; closely replicating a design-patented product silhouette; a lightly "tweaked" version of a major brand's logo (e.g. an altered Adidas logo).
  - Safer: fashion styles/patterns that follow a general trend/aesthetic without copying a specific brand's distinctive details.
  - Enforcement: takedown, 12–24 violation points deducted, permanent account lock, legal escalation.
- Product Listing & Content Policy (Risk: High) — listing images and promo videos must not mislead, violate community standards, or contain IP of unclear origin.
  - Avoid: reusing another KOL's/brand's review footage with your product spliced in; unlicensed commercial music/effects in promo videos.
  - Safer: self-produced original mockups/videos; TikTok's own Commercial Music Library.
  - Enforcement: listing suppression, reduced video reach, violation points, account restriction.

Guidance for Agent 4 (market/platform suggestion): TikTok Shop is best for trend-driven, meme/pop-culture-adjacent, youth-skewing designs riding a short fast-moving trend cycle — highest viral reach of the 4 platforms, but ALSO the highest risk of unlicensed meme/character/celebrity-likeness IP because that is precisely the content style that performs best here; enforce extra caution on celebrity/character-adjacent trend designs even when they feel "harmless meme culture".
