[TikTok Shop — US Market — Intellectual Property / Anti-Counterfeit Policy Summary]

Source: seller-us.tiktok.com/university (Intellectual Property Policy, Anti-Counterfeit Policy US), ip-brief.com — compiled from public content, last_updated: 2026-08-20.

- TikTok Shop bans all forms of IP infringement both in the listing itself AND in accompanying content: counterfeits, knockoffs, unauthorized trademarks, copyrighted media, patents, and personal publicity rights.
- Review scope is broader than Etsy/Amazon: it covers product images, descriptions, LIVESTREAMS, and VIDEO — not just static listing images (important since the BUP-01/BP-02 brief also involves video, needs to stay consistent).
- A recent update expanded enforcement against filter-evasion tricks: deliberately misspelling brand names, substituting characters/numbers to dodge automated filters (e.g. "N1ke" instead of "Nike") — the compliance checker system should watch for this pattern when OCR-reading text (not just exact-string matching).
- Penalties: product takedown, accumulating "violation points", account suspension, withheld balance, and even legal action.
- There is a dedicated program for registered rights holders (IP Protection Center) — large brands get automated counterfeit-detection tooling instead of having to search manually.

Guidance for Agent 4: TikTok Shop has the strongest proactive/automated moderation of the four (especially after the recent update), and its content audit includes video — pay special attention if the seller plans to run accompanying ad video for the listing.
