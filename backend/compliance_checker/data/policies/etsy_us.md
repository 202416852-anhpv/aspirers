[Etsy — US Market — Intellectual Property & Creativity Standards Policy Summary]

Source: https://www.etsy.com/legal/ip (Intellectual Property Policy), https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity (Creativity Standards), https://www.etsy.com/legal/prohibited (Prohibited Items Policy) — compiled from public content, last_updated: 2026-08-22.

- Intellectual Property Policy (Risk: Critical) — Etsy follows a DMCA/DMCA-like takedown process; a listing is removed as soon as a valid infringement report is received from the rights holder.
  - Avoid: unlicensed fan art (movies/shows/games/musicians/bands), sports-team branding (NFL/NBA/Premier League), celebrity likeness (image, signature, portrait), trademarked phrases/exclusive quotes.
  - Safer: original interpretations in a general artistic style without protected characters/keywords, verified public-domain material, fully original illustrations.
  - Enforcement: listing removal, warning, shop suspension, permanent ban. A seller reported repeatedly can have the whole account suspended, not just individual listings.
- Creativity Standards / "What Can I Sell" (Risk: High) — every listing must satisfy Etsy's creativity requirement: Made by seller, Designed by seller, Handpicked, or Sourced materials.
  - Avoid: using unedited commercial stock artwork with no meaningful creative contribution; reselling a POD design copied wholesale from someone else's artwork.
  - Safer: exclusively self-designed artwork; creatively combining elements into a genuinely new, personally-authored product.
  - Enforcement: loss of the Handmade/Designed badge, listing removal, shop closure.
- Prohibited Items Policy — Hate/Violence/Restricted Content (Risk: Critical) — bans designs that promote or depict hate, violence, discrimination, sexually explicit content, or exploit tragic events/disasters.
  - Enforcement: immediate listing removal, permanent account termination.

Guidance for Agent 4 (market/platform suggestion): Etsy positions itself as a marketplace for "handmade/vintage/craft" — it tolerates a light "inspired by"/fan-art lean somewhat more than Amazon (no officially published transformation-percentage threshold, assessed case-by-case), but STILL clearly prohibits directly using a registered logo/character/text without sufficient transformation. Best fit for highly personalized, originally-authored designs (name engraving, personalized gifts) and handmade-look/vintage/niche-hobby/whimsical designs aimed at gift-buyers.
