[Etsy — US Market — Intellectual Property Policy Summary]

Source: https://www.etsy.com/legal/ip/ , https://help.shopify.com (cross-reference), https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/22398703823 — compiled from public content, last_updated: 2026-08-20.

- Etsy complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) — selling products that use copyrighted text/images/artwork without the owner's permission is prohibited.
- Etsy handles infringement reports through the "Etsy Reporting Portal" — once a valid report is received, Etsy removes/disables the infringing listing and notifies the seller.
- A seller reported repeatedly can have their account suspended, not just individual listings removed.
- Etsy has a "Policy violations" page that gives sellers early warning when a listing is removed for a policy violation.
- Key difference from Amazon Merch: Etsy positions itself as a marketplace for "handmade/vintage/craft supplies" — it tolerates "inspired by"/fan-art-leaning designs somewhat more than Amazon, but STILL clearly prohibits directly using a registered logo/character/text without sufficient transformation (no officially published transformation-percentage threshold — risk is assessed case-by-case).

Guidance for Agent 4 (market/platform suggestion): Etsy is a better fit for "highly personalized, originally-authored" designs (name engraving, personalized gifts) — LOWER RISK than Amazon Merch for designs with a light fan-art lean, but still NOT safe if an original logo/character is used as-is.
