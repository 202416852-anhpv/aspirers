[Amazon Merch on Demand — US Market — Content Policy Summary]

Source: compiled from public Amazon Merch seller guides (sellersprite.com/en/blog/amazon-merch-design-compliance-checklist, merchinformer.com, merchtitans.com) — last_updated: 2026-08-20.

- Amazon does NOT allow designs that parody or resemble Amazon's own branding or trademarks.
- Review scope covers EVERYTHING: artwork, product title, description, bullet points, AND the seller's chosen brand name — a violation in any part (including text alone, not just the image) can get the listing rejected.
- Most commonly rejected: sports team logos, celebrity names, song lyrics, movie quotes, political campaign slogans — UNLESS clearly licensed.
- Keywords under tight automated monitoring (easily trigger auto-reject): "Super Bowl", "NFL", "Disney", names of major universities.
- ⚠️ IMPORTANT: Amazon Merch's content policy is STRICTER than actual trademark law — a design that could survive a real-world "fair use" defense can still get suspended by Amazon, because Amazon applies its own tighter internal standard.
- Consequence: one content-policy violation can permanently shut down the seller's account — the harshest penalty among the four platforms surveyed.

Guidance for Agent 4: Amazon Merch is the HIGHEST-RISK platform among the four surveyed for designs with a fan-art/pop-culture-reference element — only recommend this platform for 100% original designs with no trace of brand/celebrity/lyrics reference.
